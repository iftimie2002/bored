03 — Why This Matters

If you can’t prove demand,
you’re funding a belief.

In 1985, Coca-Cola replaced its formula after strong blind taste-test feedback. Consumers rejected the change in the real market, and the original formula returned within months. The lesson is simple: research must reflect real behavior, not just controlled test conditions.

Strong opinions close meetings. Market proof closes risk.

If the decision is expensive,
test it first.
